# Authored experiment source checkpoint

These are source snapshots from the ongoing Toolkit compatibility work. The manifest records exact original paths and byte hashes. Scripts retain their original fixed research paths and execution guards; this directory is an archive, not an automatic test runner.

Completed original probes and prepared or untested drafts are distinguished in their individual plans and findings. Execution reports and proprietary inputs remain outside Git at their recorded paths. The current product acceptance is documented separately under `docs/`.
